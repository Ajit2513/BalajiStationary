Supplier Payout Excel Helper

Download ready-to-use CSV sheets for raw data, brand + supplier margin tracking, EOSS scheme range management, and date-range margin difference analysis.

1) Raw Data Sheet Columns

Use this as your base sheet where transaction-level data is captured.

  • Required keys: Date, Brand, Supplier, Invoice No.
  • Value columns: Sales Amount, Cost Amount, Base Margin %.
  • Computation columns: Margin Amount, Net Payout.

2) Brand + Supplier Margin by Date Range

Keep this mapping sheet to maintain agreed margin % by period.

3) EOSS Scheme Range

Track scheme percentage adjustments applicable for a specific period.

4) Period Margin Difference Summary

Compares actual margin with configured margin and highlights differences.

Column Description
Actual Margin % (Sales - Cost) / Sales
Configured Margin % Margin from Brand + Supplier + Date range mapping
Margin Difference % Actual Margin % - Configured Margin %
Difference Amount Sales Amount × Margin Difference %
